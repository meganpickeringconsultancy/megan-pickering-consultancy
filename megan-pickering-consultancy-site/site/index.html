What I do

Four ways to work with me.

01 — Schools, Nurseries & Colleges

A direct contract, not an agency placement.

I work with schools, nurseries and colleges on a sessional or day-a-week basis, contracted directly — so you get a consistent, specialist therapist who knows your students, without agency margins or rotating cover. Covers SLT, literacy and phonics, autism support, and self-advocacy work, embedded across the setting.

  • FLEXIBLE From one day a week to termly blocks, arranged around your timetable
  • DIRECT No agency — a single point of contact from first conversation to contract
  • SPECIALIST HCPC-registered SLT, Special Needs Consultant, and published researcher
  • EMBEDDED Works alongside SENCOs and teaching staff, not in isolation

02 — Individual Clients

Highly individualised, specialist sessions.

For families seeking a single specialist who can see the whole picture — rather than separate, disconnected input from different services. Sessions are built entirely around the individual, integrating speech and language, literacy, and autism-related needs into one coherent plan rather than treating each in isolation.

  • One specialist across speech, language, literacy and autism-related needs
  • Sessions designed around the individual, not a standard programme
  • Available for a wide age range, from early years through to adulthood

03 — Expert Witness

Independent reports for tribunal and legal proceedings.

Independent, evidence-based expert witness reports, including for EHCP tribunal proceedings — assessing need, evaluating existing provision, and setting out clear, defensible recommendations grounded in current research and CPR Part 35 duties.

  • EHCP tribunal reports and evidence
  • Assessment of speech, language and communication need
  • Evidence-graded, research-backed recommendations

04 — Training

Practical training, for the people around the student.

Training sessions for educational staff, companies, and individuals — including dedicated autism training, so understanding and strategies extend beyond the therapy room into the classroom, workplace, or home.

  • Staff training for schools, nurseries and colleges
  • Autism awareness and strategy training for companies
  • 1:1 training sessions for individuals
